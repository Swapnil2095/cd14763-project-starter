## Evidence Quality Rules

Each screenshot should:

1. Clearly show the relevant AWS console page or terminal command.
2. Show the successful result or configuration being demonstrated.
3. Be readable at normal size.
4. Avoid unrelated UI where possible.
5. Contain no credentials, access keys, tokens, or secrets.
6. Use a filename that clearly identifies the rubric area.

Do not use a failed invocation as successful evidence.
